Publications

Preprint A two-scale approach for sampling local free energy minimisers Antoine Leclerc and Pierre Monmarché arXiv:2609.36195 [math.PR], 2026
arXiv pdf
summary

When a free energy has several local minimisers, the associated mean-field particle system is metastable, but with many particles the jumps between minimisers become rare events that simulations cannot reach. We couple the particles to a slow macroscopic variable that approximately follows a Langevin dynamics on a coarse-grained free energy. The empirical distribution then visits every local minimiser, with transition times that no longer depend on the number of particles. The construction is inspired by the two-scale approach to log-Sobolev inequalities for Gibbs measures.

Work in progress Social media alpha: attention, sentiment and earnings surprises ENSAE × Candriam, 2026–2027

Does social-media activity predict earnings surprises and returns beyond market and analyst signals?
